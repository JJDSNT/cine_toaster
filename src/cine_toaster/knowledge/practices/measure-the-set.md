Room dimensions, where people stand, and where cameras sit are measurements, not
adjectives. Written down once, they answer questions that prose cannot: whether a
camera fits where the plan puts it, whether two people are far enough apart to
read as two bodies, which side of the line a position falls on.

A camera outside the declared room is almost always a typo, and a typo in the
plan becomes a wrong shot several expensive steps later.
