id = "moves-keep-the-line"
title = "A moving camera stays on its side of the line; a moving character may change it"
domain = "continuity"
status = "convention"
enforced_by = ["move_crosses_axis", "subject_path_crosses_axis"]
evidence = ["SPEC-0005"]

A camera that dollies, trucks or arcs across the line of action during a shot
ends on the far side of it, and every later shot from the near side now cuts
wrong. The move looks fine alone; it only fails against the next shot.

A character walking across the line is different: it is legal, often
deliberate, and it redraws the line for every shot after it. That is why it is
a warning, not an error — the point is that the author knows the geography has
changed, not that it must not.

The line is evaluated between the two characters where they stand when the
shot begins, because that is the line the audience has in their head.
