A video model can render several shots in one generation and cut between
them itself. That keeps light, voices and the room consistent across the
cuts, which is why a run of coverage -- shot, reverse shot, insert -- is
often made as one block.

A block is one continuous piece of film. Shots that are not next to each other
in the cut cannot share it: whatever lies between them would have to be cut
out of the generation, and the continuity the block was for is lost.
