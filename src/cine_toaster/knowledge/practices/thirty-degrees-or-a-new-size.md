+++
id = "thirty-degrees-or-a-new-size"
title = "Between two shots of the same subject, move 30 degrees or change the size"
domain = "editing"
status = "convention"
enforced_by = ["jump_cut_undeclared", "chain_pose_mismatch"]
evidence = ["SPEC-0007"]
+++

Two shots of the same person from nearly the same place and at nearly the same
size do not read as a cut to a new view. They read as the picture skipping. The
classic cure is to move the camera at least thirty degrees around the subject,
or to change the shot size clearly. When the skip is the point -- a jump cut is
a legitimate device -- declare it, and the check stands down.

Size is judged where the subject stands, not where the lens is aimed: a person
at the edge of a wide two-shot is small in frame even if the two-shot's centre
is close. A subject that crosses the frame between the two shots is not a jump
either. Both were learned from a false accusation on the Echo Chamber demo,
before this rule shipped.

Chaining the last frame of one shot into the first of the next only makes sense
when the next shot starts where the last one ended. Hand a generator a first
frame from another camera and it will morph between the two.
