Most joins are hard cuts, and a hard cut needs no declaration. Anything else --
a match on a shape, a cut on the action, sound leading or trailing the picture,
a transition from the catalog -- is a decision, and a decision without a reason
cannot be reviewed later by anyone, including the person who made it.

A split edit carries sound across the join: an L-cut lets the outgoing shot's
sound run over the incoming picture, a J-cut lets the incoming sound arrive
first. Either one is only a split edit if there is something to hear on that
side. The screenplay says whether there is.
