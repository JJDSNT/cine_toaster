Who someone sounds like -- timbre, age, accent, language -- does not change
between scenes. How they sound now does: tired, hoarse, out of breath, weak
after years asleep.

When every scene describes the whole voice again, the identity drifts with
the prose. On one production, a man's slight European accent appeared in one
scene of six, because only that scene's description mentioned it.

The cast sheet holds the identity. A scene holds only the state
(`voice_state`), and a line only its delivery.

A description narrows a generated voice; it does not fix it. A video model
invents the voice again in every generation, so a character whose lines are
generated needs a recording on the sheet, which every line can be converted
to. A provider's named voice will also do, if it is the same name everywhere.
Two named voices for one person are two people, just as two master faces are.
