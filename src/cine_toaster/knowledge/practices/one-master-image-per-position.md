Costume, hair, light and background drift between generated shots unless every
shot from the same camera position starts from the same master image. Generating
a fresh starting frame per shot is the most common source of a scene that looks
almost continuous.

Not enforced yet. The project model has no field linking a camera position to
its master image, so nothing can check this and it depends on a person
remembering. This record exists to keep that gap visible rather than tacit.
