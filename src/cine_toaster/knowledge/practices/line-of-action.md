id = "line-of-action"
title = "Keep every camera on one side of the line"
domain = "continuity"
status = "convention"
enforced_by = ["axis_break", "axis_subject_missing"]
+++

Draw the line through the two characters. Every camera covering the exchange
belongs on one side of it. Cross the line and the two appear to swap places
between shots.

The wide shot decides who is on which side. When a close or an insert
contradicts the wide, mirror the insert, never the wide.

The line is declared, not inferred. Two people in a room do not always define
it, and a tool that guesses will eventually accuse a correct scene.
