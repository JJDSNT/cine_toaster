A breakdown is the promise of how the screenplay will be filmed. A line of
dialogue no shot covers is a hole in that promise, and it is usually found in
the edit, when the actor's voice runs over a shot that was never planned for it.

Coverage may overlap -- a master and its close-ups hold the same lines -- so this
only asks that each line be held by at least one shot. A line cut on purpose is
cut in the screenplay, not left uncovered.
