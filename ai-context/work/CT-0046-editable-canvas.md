# What

Plan step 13: the canvas becomes editable, and every edit is a command.

# Decisions

- **A cut decided in the runtime stands over the breakdown's.** It is kept in
  the scene's `state.json` (`cuts`, schema v4), like a chosen take, and the
  breakdown is not rewritten (ADR 0006).
  - Breakdowns can be generated by a production's own scripts (SINGULAR),
    which would erase an edit made inside them.
  - What the breakdown says stays visible (`authored_cut`,
    `authored_transition`). Clearing a decision returns to it, and the
    history keeps both.
- **Card positions are computed, not stored** (CT-0024 finding 5): the
  automatic layout draws the same film the same way, so there is nothing to
  persist or merge.
- Picture and take decisions stay where their candidates are shown, in the
  scene room and the comparison room. The canvas links to them.

# Done

- **State and loading.** `SceneState.cuts`, with `with_decision(cuts=…)`.
  `project._apply_cut_decisions` overlays each decision on the shot's `cut`
  and `transition`, so the cut records, the checks, the Cut room and
  `build` all read the decided cut.
- **Commands.**
  - `set_cut` validates the type (SPEC-0007 vocabulary), the chain and a
    catalogue transition. It refuses a scene's first shot and honours
    `expected_revision`.
  - `clear_cut` returns to the breakdown.
  - Both go through `dispatch`, with a `cut.set` or `cut.cleared` decision
    and event.
- **CLI.** `toast cut set|clear <project> <scene> <shot> [--type] [--chain]
  [--reason] [--transition --ms --transition-why] [--why]`.
- **Graph.** Cut edges carry `decided`, `authored`, the transition's
  duration and reason, and the scene revision. Scene nodes carry
  `revision`, and shot nodes `block`.
- **Canvas.**
  - `CutEditor.tsx` sends `set_cut` or `clear_cut` against the drawn
    revision and shows the runtime's refusal.
  - Shot details offer "Start the workflow for block N" when no run is
    active.
  - A decided cut is drawn thicker and marked ✎, and cut labels sit above
    the cards.
  - The selection refreshes from the reloaded graph.
- **Assistant.** It can propose `set_cut`, which is applied only after the
  person's yes.

# Validation

- `tests/test_cut_decisions.py` (4 tests) covers:
  - a decided cut is the cut, and the breakdown's bytes are unchanged;
  - clearing returns to the breakdown, with the history of both;
  - the first shot, unknown types, unknown transitions and stale revisions
    are refused;
  - the checks read the decided cut (`continuation_unchained`).
- `tests/test_assistant.py` adds the cut proposal, applied only on yes.
- The CLI was run on a demo copy: set, then clear.
- Headless canvas on a demo copy: select SC-030 P1→P2, choose Match, a
  reason and Cross Dissolve at 400 ms, save.
  - `state.json` holds the decision, with actor `canvas` (human).
  - The label reads "Match cut ✎ cross-dissolve".
  - The form offers "Back to the breakdown" and shows the breakdown's
    "Cut".
- Frontend: typecheck, build and 11 tests.
