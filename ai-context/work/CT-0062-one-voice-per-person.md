# What

The user: "Kael voice, assim como a questão dos rostos, é uma issue." This is
the voice counterpart of CT-0060. Kael's voice had been listed as a "re-check"
waiting for the author. It is an issue the system must flag.

# The issue in SINGULAR

- Kael's sheet has only a text description and no recording.
- All 46 of his lines in the cut are generated by LTX inside each clip, so
  every clip invents the voice again. One line in 3-01 is generated separately
  and mixed in at assembly.
- 1-03 and 3-01 describe his voice differently, and the scene's text wins in
  the prompt. 3-01 adds hoarseness and an accent the sheet does not have.
- The same missing recording applies to Claire, Lira, the Nurse and the
  Director.

# Done

`cast.py`, all three registered in `CHECK_CODES` and the knowledge record
`a-voice-has-one-identity`:

- `cast_voice_reference_missing` (**error**): generated speech, or speech
  mixed from a file, with no recording on the sheet and no named provider
  voice on the line.
- `voice_identity_conflict` (**error**): a scene's `voices:` differs from the
  sheet's identity. A scene that only repeats the sheet stays
  `voice_identity_restated` (advice).
- `cast_voice_split` (**error**): one character's lines name more than one
  provider voice.

Other changes:

- The demo (The Last Signal) gives Mara a reference recording
  (`cast/mara/reference/voice.wav`), made offline with Piper
  `en_US-ljspeech-medium` (LJSpeech, public domain). The sheet says it is a
  fixture whose accent is not hers.
- SPEC-0003 amendment.
